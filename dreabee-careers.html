Our values

How we work,
not just what we work on

These aren't slogans on a wall. They're how we make decisions, give feedback, and hire — including whether this role is right for you.

🔭
Transparency over politics
We share financials, strategy, and honest feedback company-wide. No hidden agendas. If you disagree, say so — loudly.
📊
Data over opinion
We build a data product, so we use data to run ourselves. Opinions welcome; evidence required to act on them.
🚀
Move with purpose
Fast doesn't mean sloppy. We ship quickly, learn from real usage, and iterate. We don't plan forever or polish unnecessarily.
🤝
Earned trust, not assumed
We give autonomy from day one and trust people to own their work. Responsibility and accountability go together.
🧠
Customer obsession
Every role touches the customer. Engineers talk to users. Designers read support tickets. Everyone reads the reviews.
🌱
Grow or stagnate
We invest heavily in learning — budget, time, and senior mentorship. The best people here get harder challenges, not just bigger titles.

Our hiring process

Clear, fast, and respectful of your time

We aim to complete the full process within 3 weeks. You'll always know what's next and get written feedback either way.

1
Apply online
CV + a few focused questions. No cover letter required. ~15 minutes.
2
Intro call
30 min with our recruiter. We tell you more; you ask anything.
3
Technical / skills screen
Role-specific. Usually async take-home (2–3 hrs) or live interview.
4
Team interviews
Meet 2–3 teammates. Values, working style, depth of experience.
5
Offer
Written offer within 48 hours of final round. References after acceptance.
