Cookie Policy

This policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use on the Dreabee platform, why we use them, and how you can control your cookie preferences.

Effective: 1 January 2025 Last updated: 14 March 2025

Last updated 14 March 2025. We've added detail on third-party analytics cookies and updated opt-out instructions.

🍪 We use cookies on Dreabee
We use essential cookies to run the platform, and optional cookies to improve your experience and understand how you use it. You can accept all, manage your preferences, or decline optional cookies.
01 — Basics

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to website owners about how their site is being used.

Cookies contain a name and value, along with metadata such as the domain they belong to, an expiry date, and flags that control how they behave. When you visit a site again, your browser sends the stored cookies back to the server, allowing the site to recognise you and recall your preferences.

📌 Cookies themselves are not harmful — they are plain text files and cannot run code or carry viruses. Their privacy implications relate to the data they store and who has access to it.

Types by Duration

  • Session cookies: Temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser. Used for things like keeping you logged in during a browsing session.
  • Persistent cookies: Remain on your device for a set period (days, months, or years). Used to remember your preferences across visits.

Types by Origin

  • First-party cookies: Set by dreabee.com directly. We control these entirely.
  • Third-party cookies: Set by external services we use (e.g., analytics tools). These are governed by the third party's own privacy policies.
02 — Cookie Types

Cookies We Use

We group our cookies into four categories based on their purpose. Essential cookies are always active; all other categories require your consent.

🔒
Essential Always On
Required for the platform to function. Without these, core features like login, security, and navigation would not work.
Examples: Authentication token, CSRF protection, session ID, load balancer routing
⚙️
Functional Optional
Remember your preferences so you get a personalised experience each time you visit. Disabling these means your settings reset each visit.
Examples: Language preference, dashboard layout, sidebar state, theme settings
📊
Analytics Optional
Help us understand how users interact with the platform so we can improve it. Data is aggregated and does not identify individuals.
Examples: Pages visited, time on site, feature usage, error tracking

Marketing Optional
Used to measure how effective our advertising campaigns are. Only set with your explicit consent. We do not use these for retargeting on third-party sites.
Examples: Ad campaign attribution, conversion tracking, referral source

Manage via Browser Settings

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies. Here's how to access cookie settings in popular browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

⚠️ Note: Blocking essential cookies will prevent core platform features from working correctly, including login and navigation.

Opt Out of Google Analytics

To prevent Google Analytics from collecting data about your visits across all websites, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
