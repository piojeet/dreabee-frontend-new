Fake Followers Impact ROI — Insights from Our Research

Before partnering with an influencer, it's essential to know how follower quality impacts your ROI. Our data science team analysed 200,000+ commercial posts and found a clear negative correlation between fake followers and campaign performance.

The takeaway? Fake followers don't just hurt reach — they directly reduce conversions, making audience authenticity mission-critical for ROI-driven campaigns.

Hero Metrics

Detecting Fake Followers: Key Metrics That Matter

At Dreabee, we've developed a suite of Influencer Hero Metrics designed to assess audience authenticity and engagement integrity.

01
Fake Follower Ratio
Measures the estimated % of followers that are inactive, bots, or purchased — ensuring audience authenticity.
02
Engagement Rate (Real vs. Expected)
Compares actual engagement with expected norms based on follower count to detect artificially inflated numbers.
03
Follower Growth Pattern
Analyses historical follower trends to uncover unusual spikes suggesting coordinated follower purchases.
04
Authenticity Score
Reviews comment content for depth, variation, and relevance to identify real interaction vs. spam bots.
05
Audience Quality Score
A composite metric evaluating follower credibility using multiple data points like activity and origin.
06
Like-to-Follower Ratio
Highlights inconsistencies between likes and follower count — can indicate low engagement or inflated followership.
