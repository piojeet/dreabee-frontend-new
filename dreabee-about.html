The Origin

We got tired of seeing brands pay for ghost audiences

In 2022, our founding team was running influencer campaigns for consumer brands in India. The results kept confusing us — high view counts, low conversions, zero ROI. When we dug in, the answer was always the same: fake followers, inflated engagement, and zero audience quality checks.

The tools that existed either cost a fortune, required complex integrations, or gave you data so vague it was useless. There was nothing purpose-built for the way real marketing teams actually worked.

So we built Dreabee. Starting with a fake follower checker that our own team used internally, we gradually built it into the full influencer intelligence platform it is today — covering discovery, analytics, campaign tracking, and brand monitoring.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Everything we build is guided by these principles — from product decisions to how we treat customers.

🔍
Data Truth First
We never round up, smooth over, or make metrics look better than they are. If a creator has 40% fake followers, we show 40%. Marketers deserve accurate data, even when it's uncomfortable.
⚡
Speed Without Sacrifice
Insights should arrive in seconds, not minutes. We obsess over performance because slow analytics tools don't get used — and decisions get made on gut feel instead.
🤝
Brands and Creators Together
We're not anti-creator. We believe authentic creators deserve to be found, fairly evaluated, and properly compensated. Our tools help the real ones rise to the top.
🌏
Built for Asia, Ready for the World
The influencer marketing tools that exist were largely designed for Western markets. We built for the nuances of Indian, South-East Asian, and MENA markets from day one.
📖
Radical Transparency
We explain our methodology. We document our data sources. We tell you when our confidence is low. We'd rather you trust us less on one data point than distrust us on everything.
🚀
Ship Fast, Fix Faster
We're a small team shipping big features. We'd rather release something imperfect and fix it based on your feedback than spend a year building in the dark.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

Q1 2022
The Problem Identified
Aryan and Priya, running influencer campaigns, discover that 35–60% of followers on campaigns they ran were fake. Start building a fake follower detector as an internal spreadsheet tool.
Q4 2022
First External Users
Share the internal tool with three brand marketing friends. 200 analyses run in the first week. Decide to build it properly.
Q2 2023
Dreabee Launches (Beta)
Launch with fake follower checker, basic engagement rate tools, and a waitlist. 1,000 sign-ups in the first month without paid advertising.
Q4 2023
Full Platform Released
Launch Influencer Discovery, Campaign Reports, and Brand Analysis. First paying customers. Team grows to 5 people.
2024
200K Creators, 8K Brands
Platform scales to 200,000+ creator profiles across Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and LinkedIn. Add TikTok ER tool and Brand Monitoring. Team reaches 8.
