General

Dreabee is a next-generation influencer marketing platform built for brands, agencies, and marketers who want real data — not vanity metrics. We help you discover authentic influencers, run end-to-end campaigns, and measure actual ROI across Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and Twitch.

Whether you're a solo brand manager or part of a large agency team, Dreabee scales to fit your workflow.

No account required for our free tools like the Fake Follower Checker and Engagement Rate Calculators. For full platform access — including influencer search, campaign management, and detailed analytics — you'll need to sign up (free, no credit card).

Dreabee currently supports Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and Twitch. We're actively expanding to Pinterest, X (Twitter), and LinkedIn in upcoming releases.

Yes! Every new account gets a 14-day free trial with full access to all Pro features — no credit card required. After the trial, choose the plan that fits your needs or continue with our limited free tier.

Influencer Discovery

Our database indexes over 50 million creator profiles across Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and Twitch. Profiles are refreshed regularly to ensure engagement rates, follower counts, and audience data stay accurate.

Dreabee offers granular filters: platform, niche/category, keywords, follower range, engagement rate thresholds, audience location/age/gender, fake follower score, brand safety flags, and past brand collaborations.

Our AI engine analyzes follower growth patterns, engagement velocity, comment sentiment, and audience quality signals to estimate real vs. bot followers. Each profile gets a 0–100 Authenticity Score — above 80 is considered highly trustworthy.

Absolutely. Create unlimited custom lists and save any influencer profile to one or more. Lists can be shared with teammates and exported to CSV or PDF for stakeholder presentations.

📊

Analytics

We calculate ER as: (Likes + Comments + Saves + Shares) ÷ Followers × 100. We average the last 30 posts for a reliable, recency-weighted score rather than a single post or lifetime average.

Top profiles (1M+ followers) refresh daily. Mid-tier (100K–1M) every 3 days. Smaller creators weekly. You can trigger a manual refresh for any profile on Pro and above plans.

Yes. Dreabee stores up to 24 months of historical data including follower growth trends, engagement rate over time, and posting frequency — accessible under the "Trends" tab on any Influencer Profile page.

Campaigns

Yes. Run multiple campaigns in parallel, each with its own influencer roster, budget tracking, content calendar, and performance dashboard. Pro plans support unlimited campaigns; Starter plans include up to 3 active campaigns.

Set a budget and target KPIs when creating a campaign. Dreabee tracks Earned Media Value (EMV), CPM, CPE, and conversion metrics (via pixel or UTM parameters). A live ROI dashboard updates as results come in, and shareable PDF reports are generated automatically.

Not currently — Dreabee is a discovery and analytics platform, not a marketplace. We don't facilitate direct payments or contracts between brands and creators. This is a feature we're exploring for a future release.

Brand Tools

Brand Monitoring tracks mentions of your brand, products, or keywords across Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok in near real-time — showing who's talking about you, mention sentiment, estimated reach, and whether the creator is an existing partner or organic advocate. Email and Slack alerts available.

Yes. The Brand Comparison tool lets you compare your brand against up to 4 competitors across influencer share of voice, sponsored content engagement rates, top collaborating creators, and audience overlap. Available on Pro and Enterprise plans.

Pricing & Plans

Three main plans: Free (limited searches, free tools), Pro (full search, unlimited lists, campaign manager, brand monitoring, API), and Enterprise (custom limits, dedicated support, SSO, white-label reporting). See our Pricing page for details.

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime — you'll retain access until the end of your billing period. Annual plans can be cancelled but are non-refundable after 30 days from the start date.

Yes! We offer 30% off for registered non-profits and an annual startup discount for companies under 2 years old with less than $1M in funding. Contact our team with proof of eligibility.

Privacy & Security

No. Dreabee uses only publicly available data — we never require influencers to log in or connect their social accounts. Our data is sourced via official platform APIs within each platform's terms of service.

Yes. Dreabee is fully GDPR, CCPA, and DPDP compliant. We process only publicly available profile data and follow strict data minimisation principles. See our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We support 2FA, role-based access controls, and SSO via Google and SAML 2.0 on Enterprise plans. Infrastructure hosted on AWS with SOC 2 Type II certification.
