Company boilerplate

Standard description

Please use this approved description when writing about Dreabee. For a shorter version, use the first sentence only.

Dreabee is an influencer marketing intelligence platform that helps brands, agencies, and marketers find, vet, and measure creator partnerships using real data. Founded in 2021 and headquartered in London, Dreabee gives teams access to 100M+ creator profiles across Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok — complete with audience authenticity scores, demographic breakdowns, and engagement analytics. Dreabee's platform is used by 12,000+ brands worldwide to reduce influencer fraud, improve campaign ROI, and make every creator decision data-driven. The company raised an £8M Series A in 2023 and is backed by leading European venture funds.
